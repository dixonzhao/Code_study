UDP002 U22B7A DESIGN NOTE
Camera Geometry Evidence Before S1 v0.3
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-08-24
Status: READ-ONLY CALIBRATION NODE

1. Trigger

The first complete U001-to-U003 Golden Run reached human review. The review
window was useful, but its machine peak at FrameCount 41481 was visually normal.
Sustained Radar loss instead began around FrameCount 41520 and ended at 41598.

After that loss, Camera 3 recovered the visually relevant forward target with
LateralPosition near zero. Camera 1 simultaneously retained a different stable
target near 40 m with LateralPosition near -2.7 m. The accepted distance-only S1
selector did not promptly restore a canonical target.

2. Confirmed Design Gap

CameraTargetContinuityArbitration v0.2 consumes only paired Distance values,
U001 validity/age and continuity state. It cannot distinguish a stable off-axis
target from the relevant forward target after state reset. B6 therefore satisfies
the original complexity stop rule for admitting LateralPosition evidence.

3. Non-Decision

U22B7A does not approve:

- an unconditional nearest-distance rule;
- a universal absolute-lateral threshold;
- Radar-assisted Camera selection;
- Camera 3 as a permanently preferred channel;
- any ground-truth target claim.

4. Intended S1 v0.3 Direction

The likely correction is geometry-assisted acquisition/reacquisition:

1. U001 validity and age remain mandatory.
2. Paired Distance and LateralPosition are bound for each Camera candidate.
3. Geometry is used to authorize or rank candidates when state is NONE or when
   reacquisition is required.
4. Distance continuity and hysteresis remain the primary within-segment identity
   evidence.
5. Ambiguity is preserved when geometry does not uniquely support one candidate.
6. Radar remains excluded from Camera arbitration.

Geometry must not automatically invalidate an already tracked target during a
turn, because the correct target may legitimately move laterally in the Camera
coordinate system.

5. Evidence Requested

For FrameCounts 41380-41780, profile:

- paired Camera 1/3 Distance and LateralPosition;
- U001 valid state;
- old S1 canonical selection and status;
- diagnostic bearing atan2(LateralPosition, Distance);
- centerline preference and advantage;
- consequence counts for several absolute-lateral thresholds.

6. Decision Boundary

S1 v0.2 remains the accepted regression baseline. S1 v0.3 is not implemented or
promoted until U22B7A real-data evidence is reviewed. The final policy parameters
must remain explicit Run Configuration, not hidden constants.

7. Next Decision

After B7A C-0, use the evidence table and threshold sweep to freeze the v0.3
Algorithm Card, controlled fixtures and S7 review-column expansion. Then implement
and replay S0-S7 against the accepted baseline.

END OF DESIGN NOTE
