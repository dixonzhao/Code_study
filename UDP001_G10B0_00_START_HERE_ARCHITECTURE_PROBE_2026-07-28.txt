1. WHY THIS PROBE EXISTS
======================================================================

G10A2 accepted:

- 706 technically eligible signals including one FrameCount key;
- 705 discovery candidates;
- 23 deterministic batches at width 32.

G10B must turn these configurations into bounded-width, FrameCount-aligned
discovery data shards for U002.

Three implementation shapes are possible:

A. Run the existing file-oriented Gate chain once per batch.
   Risk: scan the approximately 800 MB Raw CAN source 23 times.

B. Run Gate 2/3/4 globally once, materialize three all-signal long streams,
   then split into batches.
   Risk: very large intermediate disk/time amplification.

C. Build one discovery streaming engine that reuses approved U001 primitives,
   scans Raw CAN once, routes candidates to batch sinks and proves semantic
   equivalence against the accepted controlled path.

G10B0 determines which reuse boundary the real accepted source programs
actually expose.  It does not choose a statistical algorithm and does not
process company CAN values.
